🧐 How Does It Work?

Understanding the technology might sound complex, but using the app is incredibly simple. Behind the scenes, the application uses two powerful AI systems from tech giants Apple and Google:

📱 On iOS (iPhone/iPad):

Apple's Vision Framework powers the background removal. This is the same technology that powers many native iOS features like portrait mode and visual look-up. It uses a neural network trained on millions of images to identify and separate foreground subjects from backgrounds with pixel-level accuracy.

🤖 On Android:

Google's ML Kit (Machine Learning Kit) does the heavy lifting. This sophisticated AI library includes subject segmentation models that understand what a "subject" is - whether it's a person walking, a coffee mug on a table, or a bird in flight.

💡 The result: Both systems analyze every pixel, understand what's the main subject and what's the background, and then remove the background while preserving the sharpest possible edges on the subject.
